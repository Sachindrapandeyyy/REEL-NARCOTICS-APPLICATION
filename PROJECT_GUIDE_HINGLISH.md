## 1. 📌 Project Explanation (Project Kya Hai & Kyun Banaya Gaya?)

### 🛑 Problem Statement (Asli Samasya Kya Hai?)
Aaj ke time me Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat aur Facebook jaise apps ne apne platforms par **infinite vertical swipe feeds** (Reels, Shorts, Spotlight) integrate kar diye hain. Ye algorithms human psychology aur dopamine variable reward loop ko hijack karte hain:
- Ek student padhai ke liye YouTube par tutorial dekhne jata hai, lekin homepage par Shorts shelf dekhkar 2 ghante scroll karta reh jata hai.
- Ek professional college ya office ke zaroori message check karne Instagram open karta hai, aur Reels ke loop me phas jata hai.

### ❌ Traditional Blockers Kyun Fail Hote Hain?
Market me jitne bhi conventional blockers hain (jaise StayFocused, Freedom, AppBlock):
1. **Blunt Force Blocking (Poora App Band Kar Dena):** Agar aapko Instagram Reels band karni hai, to wo poora Instagram hi block kar dete hain. Result? Aapke friends ke zaroori DMs aur work chats miss ho jaate hain.
2. **YouTube Complete Ban:** Shorts block karne ke liye poora YouTube block ho jata hai, jisse students lectures, programming courses aur educational videos nahi dekh paate.
3. **Frustration & Uninstallation:** User majboori me un blockers ko disable ya uninstall kar deta hai aur wapas addiction me gir jata hai.

### 💡 Reel Narcotics Ka Solution: "The Surgical Approach"
Reel Narcotics ka funda simple aur revolutionary hai:  
**"App chalne do, lekin addictive narcotics (Reels, Shorts, Spotlight, Adult Content) ko surgically terminate karo!"**

## 4. 🚀 In-Depth Feature Explanation (Har Feature Ki Puri Jankari)

---

### Feature 1: Surgical Short-Form Video Blocker
- **Instagram Reels Detector (`InstagramReelsDetector.kt`):**
  - Instagram ke dedicated bottom tab (`reels_tab`), clip viewers (`clips_viewer_view_pager`), aur audio reels pages ko target karta hai.
  - User ke DM inbox (`direct_thread_layout`), home photo feed (`feed_composer`), aur profile pages ko unreserved chhod deta hai.
  - Interception hone par user ke app ko kill nahi karta, balki `performGlobalAction(GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK)` trigger karta hai taaki reel close ho jaye aur host app open rahe.
- **YouTube Shorts Detector (`YouTubeShortsDetector.kt`):**
  - YouTube ke `reel_watch_fragment_root`, `reel_player_page_view`, aur bottom `pivot_bar` ke Shorts tab ko inspect karta hai.
  - Regular long video player (`watch_while_layout`), search results, aur video descriptions 100% accessible rehte hain.
- **Snapchat Spotlight Detector (`SnapchatSpotlightDetector.kt`):**
  - Infinite spotlight container (`spotlight_carousel`, `vertical_view_pager`) ko identify karta hai.
  - Camera preview, bitmoji stories, aur 1-on-1 private messaging ko safe rakhta hai.
- **Facebook Reels & TikTok:**
  - Facebook watch reels aur standalone TikTok feeds ko instantaneous exit karata hai.

---

### Feature 2: Nuclear Focus Mode & Focus Lock
- **Concept:** Jab aapko padhai ya deep work ke dauran bilkul zero distraction chahiye, to aap timer set karte hain (e.g., 25 min Pomodoro ya 2 ghante).
- **Zero Bypass:** Ek baar Nuclear Mode activate hone ke baad session expire hone se pehle use pause, stop ya turn-off nahi kiya ja sakta.
- **Anti-Uninstall & Anti-Tamper Shield (`TamperDetectionEngine.kt`):**
  - Agar user phone ke Settings app me jakar Reel Narcotics ko `Force Stop`, `Clear Data`, `Uninstall` ya Accessibility turn-off karne ki koshish karta hai:
    1. System turant Settings page se user ko Home screen par kick kar deta hai.
    2. Physical haptic shock generate hoti hai.
    3. Device Admin ke zariye phone screen ko instantly `lockNow()` kar diya jata hai (touch window zero ho jata hai).

---

### Feature 3: App Lock Armor (Permanent 24/7 & Nuclear Mode)
- **Permanent Lock Mode:** Addictive games ya social media apps ko 24 ghante 7 din ke liye lock kar deta hai. Khulte hi 0ms me Home screen eject ho jata hai.
- **Nuclear Only Mode:** Selected apps sirf tab lock hote hain jab Nuclear focus session active hota hai. Session khatam hote hi apps automatically unlock ho jaate hain.
- **Visual App Picker:** Installed applications ko real-time scan karke system utility apps ko filter karta hai aur clean bottom sheet dialog provide karta hai.

---

### Feature 4: Instant Zero-Delay Adult & Browser Shield (v2.5.3)
- **Fast-Path Event Interception:** Browser (Chrome, Phoenix, UC, Mi, Vivo, Oppo) me jab bhi user URL bar ya Google search par adult keyword type karta hai:
  - Full DOM hierarchy traversal ka wait kiye bina, `AccessibilityEvent` level par sub-50ms me Home Screen eject trigger ho jata hai.
- **Unconditional Tier-1 Explicit Protection:** `pornhub`, `xvideos`, `xnxx`, `porn`, `xxx`, `hentai`, `blowjob` jaise unambiguous adult keywords kisi bhi webpage ke footer text ("Terms of Service", "Report abuse", "Privacy policy") se bypass nahi ho sakte.
- **Offline Blocklist:** Local 500+ adult domains database (`adult_blocklist.txt`) ke sath sath subdomain matching (`video.pornhub.com -> pornhub.com`) execute hoti hai.
- **Zero Cooldown Suppression:** Adult content par standard 750ms reel cooldown bypass rehta hai taaki kisi bhi rapid load par block drop na ho.

### Feature 5: Bedtime Sleep Shield & Daily Habit Loops

### ⚡ 0ms Wildcard Utility Whitelist
Emergency situations me koi bhi dialing, ambulance call ya basic utility rukna nahi chahiye. Isliye root service par **0ms Wildcard Check** laga hai:
```kotlin
fun isEssentialUtility(pkg: String): Boolean {
    if (pkg.isBlank()) return false
    return pkg.contains("dialer") || pkg.contains("incallui") || pkg.contains("telecom") ||
           pkg.contains("calculator") || pkg.contains("deskclock") || pkg.contains("clockpackage") ||
           pkg.contains("bbkclock") || pkg.contains("camera") || pkg.contains("gallery") ||
           pkg.contains("alarmclock") || pkg.contains("emergency")
}
```
Agar package inme se koi hai, to detection engine evaluate kiye bina 0 millisecond me exit ho jata hai.
